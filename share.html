How it works

  1. Pick up to 20 routes. The link updates as you go.
  2. Copy the link, or share it from your phone. Send it in a text or an email, or post it on a website.
  3. On a phone with BusNeighbor, the link opens the app with those routes on the map. BusNeighbor asks before it replaces the routes that person already has.

Questions

Does the person I send it to need the app?

To see the buses, yes. BusNeighbor is free on the App Store and Google Play, for iPhone and Android.

What happens if they open it without the app?

The link opens a page on this site that names the routes and links to both app stores. After installing BusNeighbor, they can open the link again.

Does the link track anyone?

No. The link holds only route numbers, and nothing that identifies you or the person who opens it. This page doesn't save your choices, and BusNeighbor has no accounts, ads, or analytics.
