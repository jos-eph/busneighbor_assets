How it works

  1. Pick up to 20 routes. The link updates as you go.
  2. Copy the link, or share it from your phone. Send it in a text or an email, post it on a website, or print a sign with its QR code.
  3. On a phone with BusNeighbor, opening the link or pointing the camera at the code opens the app with those routes on the map. BusNeighbor asks before it replaces the routes that person already has.

Questions

Does the person I send it to need the app?

To see the buses, yes. BusNeighbor is free on the App Store and Google Play, for iPhone and Android.

What happens if they open it without the app?

The link opens a page on this site that names the routes and links to both app stores. After installing BusNeighbor, they can open the link again.

Can I put the QR code on a flyer?

Yes. Download it as an image and add it to your flyer, or print the sign. Keep the white border around the code, and print it at least 1.5 inches (4 cm) square, larger if people will scan it from across a room.

Does the link or code track anyone?

No. The link and its QR code hold only route numbers, and nothing that identifies you or the person who opens it. This page makes the code in your browser and doesn't save your choices, and BusNeighbor has no accounts, ads, or analytics.
