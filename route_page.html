Where is the Route $route_id $vehicle right now?

BusNeighbor shows every Route $route_id $vehicle on a map, which way each one is heading, and how old its position is, with data refreshed every 30 seconds. It's free on the App Store and Google Play.

Does BusNeighbor show Route $route_id detours?

Yes. When Route $route_id is detoured, BusNeighbor shows where the $vehicle goes instead, on the map and as a card that lists the streets it takes, turn by turn.
